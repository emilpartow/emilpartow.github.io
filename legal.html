Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung

In short: this site uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it loads nothing from third parties. The only personal data involved is what the hosting provider needs to deliver the page to your browser.

1. Who is responsible

The controller in the sense of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Emil Partow. You find the address in the imprint above. For any question about your data, write to emil.partow@math.lmu.de.

2. Hosting with GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser sends a request to GitHub's servers. GitHub then processes technical data that every web request carries:

  • your IP address,
  • date and time of the request,
  • the page or file requested,
  • browser type, operating system and, if your browser sends it, the page you came from.

GitHub uses this data to deliver the site and to keep its service secure and stable. I have no access to these server logs, and I cannot link them to you.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to offer this website through a reliable and secure hosting service.

Transfer to the USA: GitHub may process the data in the United States. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Details are in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies.

Two settings can be saved in your browser's local storage, and only when you use the matching button:

  • theme: whether you chose the light or the dark appearance,
  • motion: whether you paused the animations.

These values stay on your device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else, and they contain nothing that identifies you. Saving them is necessary to provide a function you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can remove them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

4. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party content

I use no analytics tool, no tracking pixels, no social media plug-ins and no advertising. Fonts, scripts and images are all served from this site itself, so opening it sends no data to Google Fonts, a content delivery network or any other third party.

I use Google Search Console to see how the site appears in Google Search. It adds no code to this site and collects nothing from your visit; the figures are aggregated statistics from Google's own search service.

5. External links

Links to other websites, for example arXiv, Google Scholar, LinkedIn or Kaggle, are plain links. No data goes to these sites until you click one. From then on, the privacy policy of that site applies.

6. Contact by e-mail

If you write me an e-mail, I process your address and the content of your message in order to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, or Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your message concerns a contract or steps before one. My mailbox is run by LMU Munich. I delete messages once they are no longer needed, unless legal retention duties apply.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you (Art. 15),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data erased (Art. 17),
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing that is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, for reasons arising from your particular situation (Art. 21).

To use any of these rights, an e-mail is enough. You are not required to provide any personal data to use this site, and no automated decision-making or profiling takes place.
